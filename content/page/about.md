Hi, I’m Spyke.

**Spyke.space is my digital notebook.** It’s a place where I share some of my favorite things at the moment—music, writing, film, ideas, and whatever else has my attention.

These are the things I post about, but I’m also interested in the different perspectives they give me on my own life. Some things happen quickly and some things happen slowly. I never really know. When something cool grabs my attention, and I have the time to stop and capture a little of it, it’ll probably end up here.

I’m naturally curious about the world and the people in it. I love discovering new music. Lifestyle and design are pretty intimate interests for me; I love shopping and building my home and wardrobe. I tend to fixate on subjects and read nonfiction until I feel like I’ve really gotten somewhere with them. It can take me months to finish a book.

I love video games, especially the kind you can just pick up and play. I’m happiest around multiplayer arcade games, racing, and fighting games. If I’m playing alone, there’s a good chance I’m slinging spells and enthusiastically button-spamming my way through something.

I share my life with people and creatures I love. They may not always appear here, but they’re woven through the life these notes come from.

I’m pretty landlocked in Texas, though in another life I’d be surfing somewhere on the Pacific coast.

### Lately

I’m usually learning or trying something. Right now I’m learning flow arts with a pair of lighted poi, which has turned out to go wonderfully with how much I love music. I’m waiting for the right time to learn to ride a motorcycle, and there’s currently a pair of rollerblades in the mail.

I’d love to have pen pals, although fair warning: it can take me months to get anything to the post office.

Mostly, I’m glad to have a place where I can share a little of my life with friends and family and leave the door open for new friends, too.

### Say hi

I’m **@wonderspyke** around the internet. Or leave me a note here—I read every one.
